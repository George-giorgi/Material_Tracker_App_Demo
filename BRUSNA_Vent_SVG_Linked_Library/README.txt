BRUSNA — SVG TO REAL VENT PHOTO LIBRARY

Each SVG marker in /svg has a matching entry in svg_to_real_vent_photos.json.
The JSON maps the SVG filename to one or more real product-photo crops in /photos, plus model labels and source page references.

Suggested app behaviour:
- Use the SVG as the draggable/rotatable marker on the PDF drawing.
- On hover, read the matching JSON entry and show its first photo as a thumbnail.
- On click, show all photos and model names for that SVG.
- Store the selected model/photo filename with the drawing annotation.

Important accuracy notes:
- Product photos are crops from the supplied PDF brochures/product sheet; some crops may include brochure background or surrounding context.
- The Rytons brochure shows one 9x3 product photo; 9x6 and 9x9 SVGs currently link to that representative image, not a verified exact product-specific photograph.
- Generic SVG markers represent a category. They are not manufacturer logos, scale drawings, or certification of suitability.
- Respect the manufacturers' copyright and usage terms before publishing images outside your internal company app.
